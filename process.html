My SEO Process

A simple, transparent workflow so you always know what happens next.

  1. Discovery & Consultation

    Understand the client's business, audience, location, and objectives.

  2. Website & SEO Audit

    Identify issues, opportunities, and areas requiring improvement.

  3. Keyword & Competitor Research

    Research relevant search terms, competitors, and customer intent.

  4. Strategy & Implementation

    Prioritize and execute agreed SEO tasks.

  5. Monitoring & Optimization

    Review available analytics and search performance data.

  6. Reporting & Next Steps

    Provide a clear summary of completed work and recommended actions.
